# Ashwing Poker roadmap

## Short-stack decisions

Retest the 5-BB weakness against research v2 on fresh deals. Separate blind completions, small raises, and effective all-ins so the comparison shows which decisions improve. Recheck the released policy’s rising-blind performance before promoting a candidate.

## Large-pot defense

Reconstruct Hand 18 from the [Deep Thought v18 replay](https://chipzen.ai/match/bcbc3346-9ac5-4d01-a00c-a471a275da87). Compare decisions on each street across plausible value hands and bluffs. Include a profitable bluff-catch control to measure the cost of folding more often.

## Hosted validation

Collect more Chipzen matches with comparable starting stacks and blind schedules. Track both wins and chip outcomes. Use public multiway replays to test whether conclusions from the simulated mixed-table field carry over.
